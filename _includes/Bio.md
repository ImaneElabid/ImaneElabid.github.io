<h2 id="Bio" style="margin: 60px 0px 10px;">About Me</h2>

<div style="text-align: justify">
I am a researcher in distributed systems and blockchain technologies, with a PhD from the <a href="https://cs.um6p.ma/">College of Computing</a> at <a href="https://um6p.ma/">Mohammed VI Polytechnic University (UM6P)</a>. I also hold an engineering degree in Networks and Telecommunications from the <a href="https://www.ensa.ac.ma/">&Eacute;cole Nationale des Sciences Appliqu&eacute;es (ENSA)</a> of Marrakesh.

My research focuses on building trustworthy, scalable, and privacy-preserving decentralized systems. I work at the intersection of blockchain, distributed consensus, security, and data privacy, with particular interest in the fundamental challenges of trust, robustness, scalability, and regulatory compliance in distributed infrastructures.

My work combines rigorous systems research with practical algorithm design, addressing problems such as consensus efficiency, decentralized trust management, and privacy-aware blockchain architectures. More broadly, I am interested in designing distributed systems that remain secure, resilient, and efficient under real-world constraints while supporting emerging applications across digital infrastructures.

<h2 id="Interests" style="margin: 40px 0px 10px;">Research Interests</h2>
- **Distributed Systems**: Scalable algorithms, distributed consensus, fault tolerance, systems reliability, and performance optimization.
- **Blockchain and Decentralized Technologies**: Consensus protocols, decentralized trust, privacy-preserving architectures, smart contracts, and scalable blockchain infrastructures.
- **Trustworthy Computing and Security**: Privacy engineering, secure distributed protocols, trust management, reputation systems, and regulatory compliance for decentralized environments.
- **Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning**: Trustworthy AI, AI-enabled systems optimization, and the application of machine learning to secure, adaptive, and large-scale distributed systems.
